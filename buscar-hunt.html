BuscAR Hunt

A scavenger hunt platform for your whole house. Scan clues, find AR surprises with your crew, and race to crack the code.

Quick Start Full Guide (PDF) Support App Store: coming soon

The Idea

Your whole house is about to become the most fun game night you've ever had.

BuscAR Hunt turns your home or apartment into an augmented-reality scavenger hunt. Everyone joins on their own phone, races from room to room chasing written clues, scans hidden stations, and uncovers AR surprises that the whole group sees in the very same spots.

No maps. No GPS. Just your friends, your family, and your sharpest detective instincts.

Free to Join

Free to download. Free to join.

Guests never pay. If a friend is hosting, just download the app, enter the room code, and you're in the game.

Build Your Own Hunt

Write your own clues, twists and stories. Place 3D objects in real spots around your home, add your own recorded narration, and print QR station cards to place anywhere.

Story Packs

Want a ready-made adventure? The first is The Hollow House, a voiced ghost mystery with eleven stations and a vault finale, in a family-friendly take or a spookier one.

Play Together

A live leaderboard for the host, everyone sees the same AR objects in the same places, and you can rejoin any time without losing your progress.

Race or Score

Pick how a winner is chosen: race to finish, or the most points. Host up to 25 players at once.

Made for Everyone

Every line of narration is captioned on screen and saved to your Journal, with an independent voice volume control. No ads.

Great for Any Space

Works in an apartment, a big house, or a rented party space. Halloween parties, birthdays, family nights, sleepovers and game nights.

How It Works

Guests join free. Hosts unlock what they need.

Joining a hunt as a guest is always free. Building your own hunts is a one-time in-app unlock, and The Hollow House story pack is a separate one-time in-app purchase. Prices are shown in the app and the App Store.
